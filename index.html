01

RP in brief

DocumentedDerived split of US$25M

What it is for

The Project Development Objective is to improve the country's capacity to detect, report and respond to existing and emerging pathogens with epidemic potential among humans, animals and wildlife.

By March 2028, the project expects community surveillance in pilot LGUs to flag events early. It expects DOH and DA to see the same signal on a shared One Health dashboard. It expects upgraded laboratories linked to a One Health Genomics Consortium to confirm cases.

Kick-off deck, 26 Oct 2026

Why it was funded

  • The Philippines is a global hotspot for zoonotic spillover.
  • The country ranked first in the 2024 World Risk Index for disaster vulnerability.
  • The 2024 Joint External Evaluation called for stronger links across multisectoral coordination platforms.
  • The Bank's kick-off deck cites a benefit-cost ratio of 1.7 and an internal rate of return of 26%.

How a dollar moves

Recipient-executed (RETF) funds follow government systems. Bank-executed (BETF) funds never pass through DOH.

  1. The Pandemic Fund channels the grant through the World Bank as implementing entity.
  2. The Grant Agreement with the Government, through DOF, sets the terms.
  3. DOH and DA hold designated accounts: a DOH MDS account at Land Bank and a DA US-dollar account.
  4. A withdrawal application draws grant funds. DBM releases the SARO and NCA.
  5. DOH or DA procures under Bank rules, with Bank no-objection where required, and pays contracts.

BETF route: the World Bank formats the TOR, proposes the procurement setup for DOH no-objection, contracts and pays. The office reviews deliverables and gives technical clearance.

ISM wrap-up deck; kick-off deck

Where it works

  • National: digital platforms hosted by KMITS; reference laboratories at RITM and DA's ADDRL; the One Health Genomics Consortium.
  • Subnational (DOH): community surveillance pilots and point-of-care testing in Region VI (Western Visayas) and Region XII (SOCCSKSARGEN). Point-of-care testing targets six municipalities without laboratory access.
  • Subnational (DA): regional animal disease diagnostic laboratories in Regions VII and XII.

What the documents say

  • The original Gantt assumed the RETF would trigger in April 2026. It did not account for procurement time.

  • The Bank advises DOH and DA to begin building the case for a one-year extension. The writeshop guidance says not to rely on one.

What is fixed, what can move

DocumentedTiering derived

The tiers group provisions the documents state. They do not assess whether any change is advisable.

Fixed

  • Statutes: RA 11332, RA 11223 and RA 12009 (New Government Procurement Act).
  • The Grant Agreement, World Bank Procurement Regulations and the ESCP.
  • The grant amount and the results indicators.
  • The closing date. It moves only through a one-year extension.

JMC v4, hierarchy of documents

Needs World Bank no-objection

  • Any update to the POM or the AWFP.
  • RETF TORs and the procurement plan in STEP.
  • PMO TOR changes, such as the FM specialist post.
  • The JMC itself (issued).

JMC v4; ISM minutes

Within DOH and DA

  • Sequencing and grouping of consultancies.
  • TOR scope and timelines within each allocation.
  • Payment tranching and work plan annexes.
  • NPSC approval of the AWFP before Bank submission.

Writeshop minutes; JMC v4

The extension path, as currently understood

  1. World Bank, DOH and DA show progress: PMO staffed, disbursements under way, procurement launched, work plan on track.
  2. They submit a joint extension proposal to the Pandemic Fund Secretariat.
  3. Once the Secretariat agrees, DOF, DOH and DA coordinate a Government request to the Bank.
  4. DEPDev endorses the request.
  5. World Bank country management approves it.

The Bank notes that steps and timing are still to be confirmed with the Secretariat and DEPDev.
